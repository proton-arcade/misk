DESIGN RULES

Direct connections.
Deliberate steps.

The workbench models a register-centric 16-bit machine with eight general registers, 256 writable program entries, and 4,096 editable 16-bit data words (8 KiB). No shared datapath bus, clock component, pulse generator, tri-state output, or ROM is used. STEP performs one software transition; RUN repeats those transitions until halted or paused—it is not a simulated hardware clock.
